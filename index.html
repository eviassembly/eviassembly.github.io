OUR TEAM

World-class expertise
in AI and CAD.

Our team combines extensive academic research and industry experience in artificial intelligence and computer-aided design (CAD). Our members have backgrounds at institutions including ScaDS.AI, École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), Brown University, Zhejiang University, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and Beijing Jiaotong University. Our expertise focuses on world models, physical AI, information mining and related fields. We connect advanced research with practical engineering and design to help models understand how parts form a functional whole.
